1. Collaboration Charter & Master's Portfolio Objectives
This document serves as the operational contract and roadmap between Co-Researcher A and Co-Researcher B. The primary objective is to execute a high-impact, scientifically sound research study that results in a peer-reviewed publication or workshop acceptance, strengthening both researchers' Fall 2027 graduate application portfolios.
Strategic Positioning: Admissions committees at top-tier CS and AI programs evaluate applicants based on proof of original research capability, methodological rigor, clean software engineering, and collaborative leadership rather than compute budget scale. Co-authoring a polished, open-source research artifact fulfills all these criteria.
Key Collaboration Principles:
1. Equal co-authorship contribution toward the ArXiv paper and workshop submission.
2. Shared ownership of the open-source llm-society-sim repository.
3. Transparent tracking of cloud credits ($280 Modal pool) and out-of-pocket expenses ($120 cash cap).
4. Strict adherence to the 8-week completion timeline.
2. 8-Week Phased Execution Roadmap
The 8-week execution pipeline is broken down into four distinct 2-week sprints, ensuring steady progress toward paper submission:
Phase 1: Architecture & Pipeline Build (Weeks 1–2)
• Finalize repository structure (config.py, graph.py, memory.py, router.py).
• Implement async rate-limiters for Groq, Cerebras, and Google AI Studio APIs.
• Validate network topology generators (Complete, Erdos-Renyi, Watts-Strogatz).
• Milestone: Successful execution of a 20-agent dry-run across 5 rounds.
Phase 2: Matrix Execution & Probing (Weeks 3–4)
• Launch 405 simulation runs across the 15 diagnostic facts suite and 3 prompt seeds.
• Deploy Modal serverless GPU prober to compute batch token logprobs for B_i^(t).
• Collect 150 agent response samples and launch Prolific human validation tasks.
• Milestone: Complete JSON trajectory dataset generated and verified.
Phase 3: Data Analytics & Dashboard Build (Weeks 5–6)
• Run ANOVA, Kolmogorov-Smirnov, and Linear Mixed-Effects Models (LMM) in Python.
• Generate publication-ready Seaborn plot figures (TRR trajectories, BPI polarization, R0 curves).
• Construct an interactive Streamlit / D3.js web dashboard for live graph visualization.
• Milestone: Statistical significance validated (p < 0.01) and web demo deployed.
Phase 4: Paper Writing, ArXiv & Submission (Weeks 7–8)
• Write full 4–8 page research paper formatted in official NeurIPS/ICML LaTeX style.
• Incorporate mathematical formulations, literature gap table, and empirical results.
• Upload preprint to ArXiv and submit paper to targeted AI Safety / Multi-Agent workshop.
• Milestone: Official ArXiv identifier received and workshop submission confirmed.

5. Publication Strategy & Target Venues
To maximize visibility and academic credibility, the research output follows a phased release strategy:
1. Primary Workshop Submission: Target AI Safety, Multi-Agent Systems, or AI Civilization workshops at top-tier AI venues such as NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML, COLM, or ACL Findings.
2. ArXiv Preprint Release: Publish the fully formatted LaTeX draft on ArXiv simultaneously with workshop submission, establishing early claim on the research topic.
3. Open-Source Software Release: Publish the complete llm-society-sim codebase on GitHub under the MIT License, formatted with clean documentation, PyPI support, and unit tests.
4. Dynamic Interactive Web Demo: Host an interactive D3.js or Streamlit web application on GitHub Pages / Streamlit Cloud allowing visitors to simulate misinformation spread in real time.
6. Risk Contingency Protocols
To ensure project completion without delays or budget overruns, pre-planned contingency protocols are established:

```
Risk Management & Contingency Protocols

Risk 1: API Rate Limiting or Provider Downtime
• Mitigation: AsyncLLMRouter automatically fails over across Groq, Cerebras, and Gemini endpoints with exponential backoff.

Risk 2: Modal GPU Credit Exhaustion
• Mitigation: The $280 credit pool provides a 100% safety buffer over the $120 estimated probing cost. If needed, quantization (AWQ/GGUF) reduces GPU time by 50%.

Risk 3: Unexpected Statistical Non-Significance (H1/H2)
• Mitigation: Negative or neutral findings will be framed as a discovery regarding model alignment convergence rather than failure, preserving paper publishability.
```
